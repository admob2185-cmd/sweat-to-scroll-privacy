Privacy policy – Sweat to Scroll

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Short version: the app works offline, has no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything you do stays on your phone. The app doesn’t even have internet access – purchases go through the Google Play app.

What stays on your phone

Your locked apps, time bank, workout history (exercise, reps, time, minutes earned), profile choice, alarm, Scrolly’s outfit and settings are stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded, not synced and excluded from Android cloud backups. Stats, quests, badges and streak freezes are calculated from that history on the phone; the home-screen widget only shows those same local numbers.

Camera

The camera is used only while you do an exercise or the touch-grass check. Frames are analysed live on your phone by Google ML Kit models that are bundled in the app, then dropped. No photo or video is saved or sent anywhere.

Which app is open (Usage access) and the block screen

With Usage access the app checks which app is in the foreground so it can show the block screen over locked apps. With “display over other apps” it shows that screen. This information is used on your phone only, at the moment it is needed, and never stored as a history or shared.

Steps, alarm and notifications

The step challenge reads the phone’s built-in step counter (Physical activity permission) on the device. The wake-up alarm uses exact alarms and a full-screen notification. Notifications are local reminders; there is no push service. The weekly summary is a local notification on Sunday evening and can be switched off in Settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play using the Google Play Billing Library. The app only asks the Google Play app on your phone which Premium products you own; Google Play processes the payment under its own terms. No other company receives any data from the app, and we never see your name, email or payment details. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing, duels and reviews

Share cards and duel codes are created on your phone and leave it only when you send them yourself through an app you choose. A duel code contains just the nickname you typed and this week’s numbers (workouts, minutes, reps, streak, level). The app may ask Google Play to show its rating card; Google handles it and we never see your rating.

What we never do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, no account, no selling or sharing of data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything stored on your phone. We keep no copy of your data anywhere else. Your purchase history is kept by Google Play in your Google account (Google Play → Payments & subscriptions).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
